Features

Explore LizMusic features for local, cloud-connected, and offline Android listening

LizMusic is an Android personal music library player. This page explains how it works with your own local files, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, and WebDAV sources, plus playlists, local lyrics, offline cache, and connected-device controls.

Use your own files and accounts

LizMusic is a playback client. It does not provide public music, shared libraries, download links, or tools for bypassing copyright restrictions. You provide the audio files and authorize the storage account or server. Availability depends on Android permissions, the source connection, supported files, and completed offline cache.
